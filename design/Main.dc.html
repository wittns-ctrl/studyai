Design system · v3 · Creative Growth

Calm, warm and growing

A forest-green shell on an anti-glare cream canvas. Sage marks progress, gold marks the one action that matters. Flat, quiet surfaces keep cognitive load low so learning stays the focus.

Color · 60 / 30 / 10

Canvas · 60%#FDFBF7Page background, anti-glare
Forest · 30%#1B4332Sidebar, headers, hero cards
Sage · 10%#74C69DTag badges, completed ticks
Gold · 10%#E9D8A6Primary buttons, highlights
Mid forest#2D6A4FLinks, accent words, focus
Ink#1B2A22Body text on canvas

Supporting tints: sage tint #E4F1E8 (selected, AI), gold tint #F6EFD6 (needs attention), warm line #E8E3D5, ochre #D9BC66 for data fills that must read on cream. Gold is never used as text on cream.
