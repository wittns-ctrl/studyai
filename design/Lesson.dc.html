Common mistakes that lose marks
  • Saying plants get their food from the soil.
  • Calling light a reactant. It is the energy source.
  • Mixing up photosynthesis and respiration.
Real-world example Farmers light greenhouses in winter and add extra carbon dioxide. More light and more CO₂ mean faster photosynthesis, so tomatoes grow bigger, sooner.
